Server Rules

These rules help keep the community safe, fair, and enjoyable for everyone. By participating, you agree to follow them.

Use common sense

Not every harmful action can be listed here. Staff may still take action against behavior that clearly disrupts or endangers the community.

Basic Rules

Please keep these expectations in mind when playing with others. We want to have a good time!

1

Be Respectful

Treat other members with respect. Harassment, bullying, threats, hate speech, and targeted insults are not allowed.

2

Keep Content Appropriate

Do not post explicit, graphic, illegal, or otherwise unsafe content. Keep public areas suitable for the intended audience.

3

No scamming or other unfair practices

Please do not scam others, it really helps make the server more fun for everyone. Also please do not engage in inside trading or other unfair practices.

4

No Unauthorized Advertising

Do not promote other servers, businesses, social accounts, or referral links without approval.

5

Follow Staff Instructions

Cooperate with moderators during investigations or emergencies. Staff decisions may be appealed through the proper channel.

6

Report Problems Properly

Report rule violations privately with relevant evidence. Do not start public arguments, revenge campaigns, or false reports.

Instant Permanent Ban Rules

The following actions are considered severe violations and may result in an immediate permanent ban without prior warning.

⚠

Swearing

Swearing, cussing, and other derogatory language use both in and out of chat.

⚠

Illegal or Dangerous Content

Posting illegal content, malware, phishing links, or content intended to harm others.

⚠

Severe Griefing

Large scale griefing of large areas, common areas, or protected areas with the intent of chaos, destruction and player harm.

Note to griefers!

The server is protected by backups and any damage can be quickly reversed, so attempts will be futile. The server whitelist is carefully managed, and any players that violate rules may be swiftly removed from the list.

Enforcement

Consequences depend on severity, intent, previous violations, and risk to the community. Serious violations may skip directly to a permanent ban.

How to Report

Contact us (the moderators) when we're online (usually the weekends) via a message or smth in Minecraft. Obviously, give us some details so we can investigate. Please no false or unjustified reportings.
