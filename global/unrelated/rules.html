Rules for the SMP

Everyone wants to have a fun time on the server, right? By adhering to these rules, you ensure that everyone (including yourself) can have an awesome time.

General Rules

  1. Be Respectful

    No harrassing players, arguing in chat, etc.

    Punishment(s):

    1st Offence: 1h mute

    2nd Offence: 1d mute

    3rd Offence: 1d ban

  2. No swearing or inappropriate content

    Keep this server family friendly please!

    Punishment(s):

    ⚠ Note! Action taken can vary depending on severity of actions.

    1st Offence: Warning/mute

    2nd Offence: 1d ban

    3rd Offence: 7d ban

  3. No hacking, item duplication, or exploiting severe bugs/flaws

    Punishment(s):

    ⚠ Note! Action taken can vary depending on severity of actions.

    1st Offence: 1d ban

    2nd Offence: 3d ban

    3rd Offence: Permaban

  4. No spamming or abuse of chat

    No advertising other servers, excessively flushing text into global chat, etc.

    Punishment(s):

    1st Offence: Warning

    2nd Offence: 30m mute

    3rd Offence: Special moderator action

  5. Follow the unspoken PVP rules

    • Generally, don't combat log-off, or in general auto-disconnect.
    • Third parties should generally not intervene in a battle just to steal kills or team up against weak players
    • Bow-spamming or excessive use of the bow before battle is discouraged.
    • Spawn-killing is highly discouraged against.

    Punishment(s):

    ⚠ Note! Action taken can vary depending on severity of actions.

    1st Offence: Warning

    2nd Offence: Warning

    3rd Offence: Special moderator action

  6. No large-scale griefing of structures or terrain

    You may steal and raid bases but do not intentionally destroy/grief them too much (subjective). It is highly discouraged to ruin work-in-progress projects (some exceptions apply). Do not build lavacasts or other similar structures with only the intent to grief/damage terrain/builds.

    Punishment(s):

    ⚠ Note! Action taken can vary depending on severity of actions.

    1st Offence: Warning

    2nd Offence: 3d ban

    3rd Offence: 7d ban

  7. Respect staff decisions

    Do not argue in chat about staff decisions. Any appeals or survey answers should be mailed to the player 'ModBot' or be forwarded to a distributor. For allegations, please send screenshots uploaded to websites like imgbb.com and then mail the link to 'ModBot'.

    Punishment(s):

    1st Offence: Warning

    2nd Offence: Warning/mute

    3rd Offence: >1d ban or special moderator action

Feedback

How to Appeal Moderator Decisions

If you believe that a moderator decision was wrongfully made, please /mail 'ModBot' with the text '[Appeal]' at the beginning, or contact any distributor in person. You can also email 'GrepBDS@outlook.com' appeals.

How to Respond To Surveys

If you recieved a survey from 'ModBot', just reply your answer with the text '[Survey]' at the beginning. If you for some reason recieved a survey by email (Surveys are NEVER sent by email!) ignore it.
